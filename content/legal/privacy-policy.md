We collect only what we need to take, prepare and deliver orders, pay vendors and riders, and keep GoCart safe. We never sell your personal data. This policy follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and applies to customers, vendors and riders.

## 2.1 Who is responsible

GoCart, a business name of Mount Ltd (RC 9118997), Abeokuta, Ogun State, is the data controller. Contact us about privacy at support@gocartng.com.

## 2.2 What we collect

| Who | What we collect | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customers | Name, email, phone number, delivery spots (map pin, description, landmark), orders, payment references, ratings, support messages | To create your account, deliver orders, contact you about them, handle refunds and complaints |
| Vendors | Business and owner name, phone, email, shop address and map pin, CAC details (where available), bank account details, menu and prices, pharmacy licence details (pharmacies) | To verify the business, list it, send orders and pay out |
| Riders | Name, phone, email, photo, ID details, licence and bike details, bank account details, location while marked "available" or on a job, delivery history | To verify riders, match them to nearby orders, pay them and resolve disputes |
| Everyone | Device and browser type, IP address, app activity and error logs, notification settings | To run the app securely, prevent fraud and fix problems |

We do not collect card numbers; Monnify handles them. We do not collect prescriptions or medical records.

## 2.3 Our legal bases

- **Contract:** most data is needed to provide the service you signed up for.
- **Legal obligation:** tax, accounting and payment records we must keep.
- **Legitimate interests:** fraud prevention, security, improving the service and resolving disputes, balanced against your rights.
- **Consent:** push notifications and your device's location. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device or app settings.

## 2.4 Who sees what

| Recipient | What they see |
| --- | --- |
| Vendor | Your first name, items ordered and order notes. Never your phone number, address, landmark or map pin. |
| Rider (from assignment until delivery) | Your first name, phone number, map pin, delivery description and landmark, delivery status |
| Customer | The assigned rider's first name and phone number; the vendor's shop name and location |
| Service providers | Only what each needs to do its job (section 2.5) |
| Authorities | Only when Nigerian law requires it, or to protect someone's safety |

Riders and vendors may use customer details only to complete the order, and must not keep or reuse them.

## 2.5 Service providers

We use trusted providers who process data on our instructions: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Vercel (hosting), Monnify (payments and payouts), Resend (email), Google (sign-in and road distances), Mapbox (maps) and browser push services (notifications). Some of them store data outside Nigeria. Where they do, we rely on safeguards the NDPA recognises, such as contractual protections.

## 2.6 Location

- **Customers:** we use your device location only when you tap "Use my location", to place your pin. We store the delivery spots you save.
- **Riders:** while you are marked available, the app shares your location about every 2 minutes so vendors can find the nearest rider. Location is not collected when you are unavailable. Job locations are kept for 90 days for disputes; availability updates are overwritten.

## 2.7 How long we keep data

| Data | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Account details | While your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of closure |
| Orders, payments, payouts, refunds | 6 years, for tax and accounting |
| Rider job locations | 90 days |
| Saved delivery spots | Until you delete them or close your account |
| Support messages | 2 years |
| Logs | Up to 90 days |

## 2.8 Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, get a copy in a portable format, object to or restrict certain uses, and withdraw consent. Email support@gocartng.com; we respond within 30 days. Some records must be kept by law even after you close your account. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## 2.9 Security

Access to personal data is limited to people and systems that need it. Data is encrypted in transit. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay.

## 2.10 Messages we send

We send emails and notifications about your account and orders. These are part of the service and cannot be switched off while you have an account, except push notifications, which you control. We will send marketing only if you agree, and every marketing message lets you opt out.

## 2.11 Cookies and storage

GoCart uses essential cookies and your browser's storage to keep you signed in and remember your cart and delivery spot. We do not use advertising cookies.

## 2.12 Children

GoCart is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 2.13 Changes

We will notify you in the app or by email before material changes to this policy take effect.
