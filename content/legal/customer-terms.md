## 1.1 What GoCart is

GoCart is a platform that lets you order from independent restaurants, supermarkets and pharmacies ("vendors") in our delivery areas, and arranges delivery of your order by independent riders. The vendor sells the items and is responsible for them. GoCart is responsible for running the platform, taking payment and arranging delivery.

## 1.2 Your account

- You must be at least 18, or use GoCart with the permission of a parent or guardian.
- You sign in with Google or a code sent to your email. Keep access to your email secure; anyone who can open it can sign in as you.
- Give us a phone number you answer. Riders use it to reach you during delivery.
- One account per person. You are responsible for orders placed on your account.

## 1.3 Orders and prices

- Vendors set their item prices and decide what is available. Photos are a guide; the item you receive may look slightly different. Prices on GoCart may be up to 5% higher than in the vendor's shop.
- Your order is placed only when your payment is confirmed. Unpaid orders expire after 30 minutes.
- A vendor may reject an order, for example if an item has run out. If a vendor does not respond within 10 minutes, the order is cancelled automatically. In both cases you get a full refund (section 1.8).

## 1.4 Fees

Before you pay, checkout shows every charge. There are no hidden fees.

| Charge | How it is set |
| --- | --- |
| Items | The vendor's prices |
| Delivery fee | Based on the road distance between the vendor and your delivery spot |
| Service fee | 5% of the item total, minimum ₦100, maximum ₦500 |

## 1.5 Payment

- Payments are processed by Monnify, a licensed payment provider. GoCart does not see or store your full card details.
- We may refuse or reverse a payment we reasonably believe is fraudulent.

## 1.6 Delivery

- We deliver only to spots inside our current delivery areas. You must place your map pin on the right spot and describe the place clearly; a wrong pin or description can delay or prevent delivery.
- Delivery times shown are estimates, not guarantees.
- Your rider may call you. Each order has a delivery PIN: give it to the rider only once your order is in your hand. Giving the PIN confirms you received the order.
- For pickups at the OOUTH gate, the rider waits at the gate for up to 10 minutes after telling you they have arrived.

## 1.7 If the rider cannot reach you

If the rider waits 10 minutes at your delivery spot and cannot reach you by phone, the order is marked "customer did not show". Food and other perishable items are not refunded. For other items we will contact you to arrange a new delivery (with a new delivery fee) or refund the item total, less the delivery and service fees.

## 1.8 Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel free of charge until the vendor accepts your order. Once the vendor has accepted it, the order cannot be cancelled.

| Situation | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Vendor rejects the order, or does not respond in 10 minutes | Full refund of everything you paid |
| Order not delivered because of the vendor or GoCart | Full refund |
| Items missing or wrong | Refund for those items (and fees, if the whole order was wrong) |
| Item damaged in delivery | Refund for that item |
| You change your mind after the vendor accepts | No refund; the vendor has started preparing it |

- Report problems through support within 2 hours of delivery for food, or 24 hours for other items, with a photo where possible.
- Refunds go to your GoCart credit straight away. You can use it on any future order. Refunds are not sent to your bank account or card.

## 1.9 GoCart credit

GoCart credit comes only from refunds and promotions. You cannot top it up, transfer it or exchange it for cash, except that unused credit from refunds is paid to your bank account if you close your account. Promotional credit may have an expiry date, which we will show you.

## 1.10 Food, allergies and pharmacy products

- Vendors prepare food. GoCart cannot guarantee any item is free from allergens; if you have an allergy, check with the vendor before ordering.
- Pharmacies on GoCart sell **over-the-counter products only**. Prescription-only medicines are not sold on GoCart. Read the label and follow the dosage; nothing on GoCart is medical advice. GoCart does not sell alcohol, tobacco or illegal drugs.
