This agreement is between GoCart, a business name of Mount Ltd (RC 9118997), Abeokuta, Ogun State ("GoCart"), and you, the rider. You deliver GoCart orders as an independent contractor, when you choose, and are paid for each completed delivery.

## 4.1 You are independent, not an employee

- You decide when to be available. There are no fixed hours, shifts or minimum number of jobs.
- You provide your own motorcycle, fuel, phone and data, and pay your own costs and taxes.
- You may do other work, including normal okada work, when you are not carrying a GoCart order.
- Nothing in this agreement makes you an employee, agent or partner of GoCart.

## 4.2 What you need

- Be at least 18, with a verified NIN and a valid motorcycle riding licence.
- A roadworthy motorcycle with valid papers, and a helmet you wear on every job.
- A smartphone with the GoCart app, location and notifications turned on.
- A bank account in your own name for payouts.
- Obey traffic laws, including any Ogun State rules on commercial motorcycles.

## 4.3 Being available

When you tap "I'm available", the app shares your location about every 2 minutes so vendors can see the nearest riders. If the app gets no location for 30 minutes, you are marked unavailable. Tap "I'm not available" when you stop.

## 4.4 Taking a job

- A vendor may call you about a pickup. Agree only if you can go straight there.
- Once the job is assigned to you in the app, go directly to the vendor. Do not pass the job to anyone else.
- If something stops you completing a job, tell GoCart immediately.

## 4.5 Carrying orders

- Collect only the order shown in your app. Keep it sealed and never open or tamper with it.
- Carry it safely and upright, in a delivery bag where provided.
- We advise you not to carry a passenger while carrying a GoCart order. If you do, you remain fully responsible for the order, and neither you nor your passenger may open, touch or tamper with it.

## 4.6 Delivering and the PIN

- Go to the customer's map pin. Call them if you cannot find the place.
- Ask for the delivery PIN only once the order is in the customer's hand. Never mark an order delivered without the correct PIN.
- **If the customer does not show:** tap "I've arrived", call them, wait 10 minutes, then tap "Customer didn't show" and follow GoCart's instructions for the order. At the OOUTH gate, wait at the gate.

## 4.7 Pay

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery pay | The amount shown in the app for each job, based on the distance from vendor to customer |
| When you earn it | For each delivery completed with the PIN, and for "customer didn't show" jobs where you followed section 4.6 |
| Cancelled after you reach the vendor | ₦200 for your trip. Customers cannot cancel once the vendor has accepted, so this applies only when the vendor or GoCart cancels |
| Payout | Daily at 10:00 PM, to your own bank account, covering jobs since the previous payout |
| Cash | Customers pay through the app. Never collect cash for an order or ask for extra money |

- If an order is lost or damaged through your fault, GoCart may deduct up to the order's value after looking into it. You may dispute any deduction within 48 hours.
- GoCart may hold a payout for up to 7 days while it investigates suspected fraud, and will tell you why.
- GoCart will give you at least 7 days' notice before changing how delivery pay is calculated.

## 4.8 How you must behave

Be polite to customers, vendors and GoCart staff. Never harass, threaten or overcharge anyone. Never work under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

## 4.9 Customer information

You see a customer's first name, phone number and location only to complete their delivery. Do not save their number, contact them afterwards, or share their details with anyone.

## 4.10 Safety and accidents

- Ride safely and within the law. Your safety comes before any delivery time.
- GoCart does not currently provide insurance for riders. Report any accident to GoCart immediately.
- GoCart is not responsible for injury, loss or damage while you ride, except where caused by GoCart's own negligence.

## 4.11 Liability

You are responsible for fines and traffic offences, and for losses caused by your own fault or misconduct. You will cover GoCart for claims arising from them.

## 4.12 Ratings, suspension and ending the agreement

- Customers and vendors may rate deliveries. Consistently low ratings may lead to a review.
- Either side may end this agreement at any time.
- GoCart may suspend or remove you immediately for theft, fraud, tampering with orders, marking orders delivered without the PIN, abuse, unsafe riding or repeated complaints.
- When the agreement ends, GoCart pays what it owes you at the next payout.

## 4.13 General

- GoCart may update this agreement with at least 7 days' notice in the app. Continuing to take jobs after that means you accept the changes.
- Disputes: contact GoCart first; if unresolved, mediation in Ogun State; then the courts of Ogun State. This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

## Digital acceptance

By ticking the box and submitting registration, you confirm that you have read and understood this agreement, agree to it voluntarily, are at least 18, hold a valid motorcycle riding licence, and that the information you gave is true.
