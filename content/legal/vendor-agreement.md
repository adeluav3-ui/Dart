This agreement is between GoCart, a business name of Mount Ltd (RC 9118997), Abeokuta, Ogun State ("GoCart"), and the business registering on GoCart ("you"). It has core terms that apply to every vendor, plus one schedule for your type of business: **Schedule A** (restaurants), **Schedule B** (supermarkets and stores) or **Schedule C** (pharmacies). If the core terms and your schedule conflict, the schedule wins.

## 3.1 What you agree to do

You make your products available to customers through GoCart, keep your listing accurate, and prepare and hand over orders as set out below. GoCart lists your business, takes payment from customers, arranges delivery by GoCart riders and pays you.

## 3.2 Your listing

- Your business details, opening hours, menu or product list, photos and prices must be accurate and kept up to date.
- Prices on GoCart may be at most 5% higher than your in-store prices, and no more.
- Mark items unavailable as soon as they run out. Mark your shop closed when you are not taking orders.
- You confirm you own or have permission to use every photo and description you upload.

## 3.3 Taking orders

- While your shop is open on GoCart, keep the GoCart dashboard open with notifications on.
- Accept or reject each order within 10 minutes. After 10 minutes without a response, the order is cancelled automatically and the customer is refunded.
- Reject an order only if you genuinely cannot fulfil it. Do not swap items without the customer's agreement.
- Repeated missed or rejected orders (for example, 3 or more in 7 days) may lead to your shop being paused.

## 3.4 Packaging and handover

- Pack every order securely and sealed, and mark it ready in the app only when it is ready.
- Hand the order only to the rider assigned in the app.
- Your responsibility for an order ends when it is properly handed over, correctly prepared and packed, except for problems caused by your products or your negligence.

## 3.5 Commission and payouts

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Commission | 5% of the item total of each delivered order |
| Not commissionable | Delivery fees and service fees. These are paid by the customer to GoCart, not to you |
| Payout time | Daily at 10:00 PM, covering orders delivered since the previous payout |
| Payout account | The verified bank account you registered, in your business's or owner's name |
| Deductions | Commission, plus any refunds caused by you (section 3.6) |
| Statement | Every order, commission and deduction is shown in your dashboard |

- Bank processing can sometimes delay arrival after 10:00 PM. GoCart is not responsible for delays caused by banks or payment providers.
- GoCart may hold a payout for up to 7 days while it investigates suspected fraud, and will tell you why.
- If deductions exceed a day's earnings, the balance is deducted from later payouts.
- You are responsible for your own taxes.
- GoCart will give you at least 30 days' notice before changing the commission rate.

## 3.6 Refunds caused by you

When a customer is refunded because of you (wrong or missing items, poor quality, a rejected order after preparation, or a breach of your schedule), the refunded item amount is deducted from your payout. If the whole order was unusable, the fees refunded may also be deducted. GoCart reviews the evidence before refunding. You may dispute a deduction through support within 48 hours.

## 3.7 Customer information

- You see only the customer's first name, the items ordered and any order notes. You never receive their phone number, address or location.
- Use customer information only to prepare the order. Do not contact customers outside GoCart, and do not keep, share or sell their information.

## 3.8 Delivery

GoCart provides and manages the riders; you do not pay them. GoCart is responsible to customers for delivery problems after handover, such as lateness, damage in transit or rider conduct. Report any rider problem to GoCart.

## 3.9 Quality and the law

You are responsible for the quality, safety, legality and correct description of everything you sell, and for holding any licences or permits your business needs. Alcohol, tobacco and vaping products, illegal drugs, and anything else illegal to sell in Nigeria may not be listed or supplied on GoCart. Your schedule sets out the specific standards.

## 3.10 Liability and indemnity

- You will cover GoCart for any claim, loss or fine caused by your products, your preparation, your premises or your breach of this agreement.
- GoCart's total liability to you under this agreement is limited to the commission it earned from you in the 3 months before the claim.
- Neither side is liable for indirect losses or for events outside its reasonable control.

## 3.11 Your name and photos

You allow GoCart to use your business name, logo, photos and menu on GoCart and in GoCart's marketing while you are a vendor.

## 3.12 Suspension and ending the agreement

- Either side may end this agreement with 7 days' notice.
- GoCart may pause or remove your shop immediately for fraud, a food or product safety risk, counterfeit or prohibited products, abuse of customers or riders, or repeated failures.
- When the agreement ends, GoCart pays what it owes you, less commission and refunds, at the next payout.

## 3.13 General

- "Partner" describes the business relationship only. This agreement does not create a legal partnership, joint venture or employment.
- GoCart may update these terms with at least 7 days' notice (30 days for commission changes). Continuing to sell on GoCart after that means you accept the changes.
- Disputes: contact GoCart first; if unresolved, mediation in Ogun State; then the courts of Ogun State. This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
- You confirm the information you gave at registration is true and that you are authorised to bind the business.

## Schedule A — Restaurants

1. Prepare food in clean conditions, with clean utensils and staff who follow basic hygiene (washed hands, covered hair, no handling food while ill).
2. Store raw and cooked food separately, at safe temperatures. Do not sell food that has gone bad or is left over from a previous day as fresh.
3. Use clean, food-safe, leak-proof packaging, sealed so tampering can be seen.
4. Tell customers who ask about ingredients and common allergens (for example groundnuts, egg, fish, shellfish, milk).
5. Comply with environmental health and food hygiene requirements, and hold any permits required by Ogun State or your local government.
6. You are responsible for food poisoning, contamination, foreign objects and wrong or missing items caused before handover.

## Schedule B — Supermarkets and stores

1. Sell only genuine products. Products that require NAFDAC registration must be registered.
2. Do not supply expired products. Tell the customer before supplying any product that expires within 7 days.
3. Keep chilled and frozen products at the right temperature until handover, and pack them to stay cold.
4. Do not list alcohol, tobacco, vaping products, illegal drugs or any product that requires an age check. GoCart riders cannot check ages.
5. If an item is out of stock, reject it or the order. Do not substitute without the customer's agreement.
6. You are responsible for expired, counterfeit, defective or incorrectly supplied products, and damage before handover.

## Schedule C — Pharmacies

1. Your premises must be registered with the Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN), with a superintendent pharmacist, and you must keep your licences current. You will give GoCart your licence details at registration and when they are renewed.
2. **List and supply only over-the-counter products, and health, wellness and personal-care items.** Do not list or supply prescription-only medicines or any controlled substance, including any product containing codeine or tramadol. GoCart may remove any listing at its discretion.
3. Supply only genuine products registered with NAFDAC where required, within their expiry dates, in original packaging with their label and leaflet.
4. Store and pack temperature-sensitive and fragile products correctly. Use tamper-evident packaging.
5. Refuse and report to GoCart any order that looks like misuse, such as unusually large quantities.
6. Treat what customers buy as confidential health-related information.
7. You are responsible for the authenticity, quality, storage, expiry and correct supply of products.
